# GSAT AI

AI-powered SAT study platform.

## About

GSAT AI is an intelligent study platform designed to help students prepare for the SAT exam. It leverages artificial intelligence to provide personalized learning experiences, adaptive practice questions, and performance analytics.

## Features (Planned)

- **AI-Powered Practice Questions** - Adaptive question generation based on student performance
- **Personalized Study Plans** - Custom study paths tailored to individual strengths and weaknesses
- **Performance Analytics** - Detailed dashboards tracking progress across SAT sections
- **Reading & Writing** - Comprehensive practice for the Evidence-Based Reading and Writing section
- **Math** - Targeted practice for both Calculator and No-Calculator math sections
- **Vocabulary Builder** - AI-curated vocabulary lists with contextual learning
- **Timed Practice Tests** - Full-length and section-specific timed simulations
